Hierarchical Object Perception — two independent paths

Novel road objects are never dropped: the system abstracts each detection up a semantic taxonomy to the most specific level it can safely justify, then a second, independent segmentation path cross-checks it. Pick a scene, toggle the segmentation overlay, and click a detection to see its taxonomy descent and the cross-check verdict.
